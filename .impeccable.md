## Design Context

### Users
Small and midsize businesses that sell or serve customers through social comments and DMs. They need fast replies, qualified leads, and a clear path for human follow-up.

### Brand Personality
Approachable, capable, and attentive. The product should make busy teams feel that conversations are under control and customers are cared for.

### Aesthetic Direction
Clean, photographic, and conversational. The creator supplied Nura as the layout and motion reference: an inset moving-image hero, centered display type with an italic serif accent, quiet light panels, an editorial image-and-UI overview, and a compact performance bento. Reply2Lead keeps its name, green status color, recognizable social marks, and functional conversation demos.

### Design Principles
1. Show the product through concrete conversations and functional state changes rather than abstract AI language.
2. Give the main call to action a clear, consistent location.
3. Use generous spacing and sharp type hierarchy to make a long story easy to scan.
4. Bring human handoff and team control into the visual story, including Ray pausing when a person takes over.
5. Keep mobile navigation and core information accessible.
6. Motion must visibly explain a product state and settle in a meaningful result.
