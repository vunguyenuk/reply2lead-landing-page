## Design Context

### Users
Small and midsize businesses that sell or serve customers through social comments and DMs. Owners and small teams need useful replies after hours, timely follow-up, qualified leads, and a clear path for human takeover. They are evaluating whether the value of recovered conversations justifies the total cost.

### Brand Personality
Approachable, capable, attentive, and commercially direct. The product should feel like a dependable sales teammate in text conversations, while making clear when a human takes over.

### Aesthetic Direction
The current visual source of truth is Figma frame `250:2` in `Dtq76I0bNV7JgCFnIbZ6uX`. It uses a white canvas, inset editorial photography, dark charcoal chapters, vivid green accents (`#25d366`), and Inter Display for all page and interface text, with the font's natural character spacing. Desktop content is centered at a 1440px maximum width. Preserve the captured product details and purposeful state animations while matching that frame.

### Design Principles
1. Show the product through concrete conversations and functional state changes rather than abstract AI language.
2. Give the main call to action a clear, consistent location.
3. Use generous spacing and sharp type hierarchy to make a long story easy to scan.
4. Bring human handoff and team control into the visual story, including Ray pausing when a person takes over.
5. Keep mobile navigation and core information accessible.
6. Motion must visibly explain a product state and settle in a meaningful result.
7. Explain the sales result, then show the conversation and follow-up mechanism. Use actual Reply2Lead evidence for numerical claims and pricing.
