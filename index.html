FAQ

Know exactly how Ray works.

Ask us something ↗
What can Ray answer?+

Ray can answer routine questions using the product, service, FAQ, and policy information you provide.

What happens when Ray does not know?+

Ray flags the missing answer and brings your team into the conversation. You can save the right response or keep that question with a person.

Do I need to write a sales playbook?+

No. Add your website, FAQs, policies, files and past chats. Ray shows what it knows and asks about gaps. Test it with real buyer questions, then approve answers and handoff rules before launch.

What happens when a lead goes quiet?+

Ray can keep the context and follow up at the agreed time, so a promising conversation does not disappear when your team logs off.

How do I judge whether Ray is worth the cost?+

Ask for the monthly price and any usage or channel fees. Compare the full cost with the profit from one extra sale or booking and the time your team spends on repeat questions.
